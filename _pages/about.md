Hello there! Welcome to my corner of the digital world. My name is Behzad (بهزاد).
I work on artificial intelligence with a focus on large language models, particularly post-training, including SFT, RL, reasoning, and evaluation. I come from a machine learning background, with work spanning theory, experimentation, and deployed systems.

I'm currently working at Google DeepMind as part of the GenAI group. Prior to this, I was part of the NLP Signals team at Twitter‘s Cortex team building Tweet representations. And before that, I was a researcher at Megagon.ai doing NLP research. I got my PhD in Computer Science from Boston University back in 2016.
